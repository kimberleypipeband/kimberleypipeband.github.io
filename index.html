The night

A hundred years, played live.

A tattoo is a night of pipes, drums and marching music, staged for a crowd. On July 24, sixteen bands, dancers and special guests fill the arena for the Kimberley Pipe Band’s 100th birthday.

Our century

Two brothers. One parade. A hundred years.

Brothers Angus and Hamish Scott started the band in 1927. Angus was its first Pipe Major.

Since then the pipes and drums have led Kimberley’s parades, played at its celebrations and stood with it in remembrance. This Tattoo thanks the musicians, families, volunteers and supporters who carried the band this far. It welcomes everyone who will carry it next.

    16 ways to help

    Roles still open for July 23 and 24, 2027. Duties and times may change as planning continues.

    Friday, July 23

    • Welcome Dinner Team LeadersAdvance planning and all day Friday

      Coordinate preparations, assign duties, and oversee setup, service and cleanup. Work with the chef to keep the evening running smoothly.

    • Table and Chair SetupFriday morning

      Arrange tables and chairs at the Kimberley Curling Club to the dinner layout. Keep aisles, exits and serving areas clear so the room is ready for decorating and service.

    • Server AssistantsFriday, 3:30–7:00 p.m.

      Set tables, serve food, restock supplies, clear dishes and tidy the dining area. Welcome visiting performers and guests.

    Saturday, July 24

    • Parade Team LeadAdvance planning and Saturday morning

      Coordinate arrangements with participating bands and local partners. Organize staging, the schedule and volunteers, and secure permits from the Department of Highways and the City.

    • Parade Staging and Route VolunteersSaturday morning

      Help bands find their assembly areas, keep parade order and guide participants along the approved route. Pass traffic or safety concerns to the parade lead.

    • UshersSaturday, 4:30–7:00 p.m. · Western Financial Place

      Help patrons find seats, answer venue questions and keep aisles clear. Assist guests with accessibility needs and refer concerns to venue staff.

    • Security SupportSaturday, 5:30–9:30 p.m. · Western Financial Place

      Monitor designated access points and direct people to authorized areas. Report concerns promptly to venue staff or security personnel and follow their instructions.

    • PhotographerSaturday, July 24 · Parade and Tattoo

      Photograph the parade and the Tattoo at Western Financial Place.

    Both days

    • Band HostsFriday and Saturday

      Be the friendly local contact for one visiting band. Share schedules, directions and event information, and help the band reach assembly areas and activities on time.

    Behind the scenes

    • Accommodation CoordinatorLead-up to the event

      Gather accommodation needs from visiting bands, liaise with local providers, share booking information and track arrangements.

    • Sponsorship and FundraisingOngoing through the event

      Identify and approach sponsors, help with fundraising, follow up on commitments and make sure sponsors get the recognition agreed.

    • Grant Applications and SourcingMostly before the event, with follow-up

      Research grants, prepare applications with committee input, track deadlines and gather information for funding reports.

    • Communications and FinanceOngoing, including wrap-up

      Support correspondence and recordkeeping, track income and expenses, organize invoices and receipts, and prepare financial summaries.

    • Website and Social MediaOngoing until the event

      Keep event information current online and prepare approved posts about performers, tickets, sponsors and volunteer opportunities.

    • Poster DistributionJune 2027, with follow-up

      Deliver and display posters at approved locations in Kimberley, Cranbrook and nearby communities. Keep a distribution list and remove posters afterward where required.

    • Mine Portal Construction and SetupBefore the event, setup and takedown

      Plan, build, transport and install the mine portal display, coordinate placement with the venue and help remove it afterward.
