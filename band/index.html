Kimberley Pipe Band

Piping and drumming in Kimberley since 1927

Parade season is here.

Kimberley Pipe Band is committed to playing in the local parades for the summer. Creston Blossom Fest is the start of the parade season for us. What a great crowd came out to watch! Next up is Marysville Days on June 6. We will be playing at the Kimberley grad ceremonies for the class of 2026 on June 13.

Don’t forget to bring the family out to the Spirit of the Rockies Parade in Cranbrook on June 20 and cheer on the pipe band and many other floats. And of course, don’t forget about Julyfest on July 18.

Thank you for your many years of support.

100th Anniversary Tattoo 2027

2027 will mark the 100th anniversary of the Kimberley Pipe Band. We are underway with the planning, with a great group of volunteers. The hard work has begun to bring you an incredible weekend of fun on July 23–24, 2027. Stay tuned, we look forward to the show.

Visit the Tattoo site

The Tartan: Cameron of Erracht

A blend of the MacDonald and Cameron tartans, devised by Marsali MacLean, mother of Sir Allan Cameron of Erracht. In 1793 he raised the 79th Highlanders, which became the Queen’s Own Cameron Highlanders.
