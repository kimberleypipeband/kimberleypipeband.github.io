KIMBERLEY PIPE BAND WEBSITE
===========================

Four pages:
  index.html    Home: where we're playing, the 100th Tattoo, ways in
  story.html    Our story: Pipe Majors, history timeline, members, past members
  book.html     Book & join: bookings, booking form, lessons, practices, joining
  listen.html   Listen & watch: recordings, videos, recent photos

TO UPDATE THE SITE, YOU USUALLY ONLY NEED ONE FILE:
  js/site-data.js
    - upcoming events (dates disappear by themselves once past)
    - members and their Member Monday answers
    - recordings
    - YouTube codes for the videos
    - the Tattoo link and band email
  Open it in Notepad, follow the notes at the top, save, refresh.

PHOTOS
  img/members/   current members (name-surname.jpg)
  img/past/      past members wall
  img/history/   photos used in the timeline
  img/gallery/   video posters and recent photos

COLOURS AND FONTS
  css/site.css (top of the file)

TO PUT IT ONLINE
  This whole folder goes in the GitHub repo as "official_band_site".
  The site's address will be www.kimberleypipeband.ca/official_band_site/
