Join the band

Pipers, side drummers, tenor and bass drummers. No experience needed.

Our newest players started learning at 8 and at 40. Some of us have been in the band for close to fifty years, some for one. Everyone started with a practice chanter or a pair of sticks.

  1. Say hello

    Email the band or call one of our teachers. Tell us whether it’s pipes or drums.

  2. Start lessons

    Pipers start on a practice chanter, drummers on a practice pad. We’ll point you to what you need.

  3. Come on a Monday

    Sit in on practice, meet the band, and work toward your first parade.
